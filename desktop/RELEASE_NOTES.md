The latest SpinDeck for Mac, built automatically from the `main` branch. It runs on Intel and Apple Silicon Macs.

**Install**
1. Download **SpinDeck.dmg** below and open it.
2. Drag **SpinDeck** into **Applications**.
3. The first time, macOS will say SpinDeck "can't be opened" or is from an unidentified developer, because it isn't signed with a paid Apple Developer account. Allow it once:
   - Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway** next to the SpinDeck message.
   - On older macOS versions, you can instead right-click SpinDeck in Applications and choose **Open**.

After that, SpinDeck opens like any other app.

**What's different from the website**
- Your music and both decks come back when you reopen the app, with no reconnect clicks.
- A DJ controller (e.g. the DDJ-FLX4) connects without a permission prompt.
- **File → Add Music Files… (⌘O)** and **Add Music Folder… (⇧⌘O)**. You can also drop songs on the window or on the Dock icon.
