DJ controller

Plug in your controller, then press Connect.

    How to map a controller
    1. Press Connect controller and allow MIDI access.
    2. Press MIDI Learn. Every mappable control gets an outline.
    3. Click a control on screen (for example Deck A ▶), then press or move the matching button, knob, fader or jog wheel on your controller.
    4. Repeat for each control. Esc cancels; right-click or Shift-click a mapped control to clear it. Press Done when finished.
    5. Export saves your mapping as a file you can share with people who have the same controller.

    Knobs and faders use soft takeover: they start moving once the hardware passes the on-screen position.
